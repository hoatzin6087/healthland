Your Wellness Destination in Deira, Dubai

Health Land Spa is a premium spa room massage and wellness service for visitors to Marco Polo 4-star hotel in Al Muteena, Deira, Dubai. Our spa room is located on the first floor for a comfortable and private treatment experience.

Our experienced therapists offer Ayurvedic, aroma, Swedish, Thai and full body massage, as well as Moroccan bath and Jacuzzi bath experiences. Each service begins with a consultation so the session can be adapted to your comfort and wellness goals.

Our 60-minute promise

Every service is planned as a complete 60-minute spa room session inside Marco Polo 4-star hotel, giving you enough time to settle in, enjoy the treatment and leave feeling restored. Call or WhatsApp us to confirm availability and choose the right treatment.
